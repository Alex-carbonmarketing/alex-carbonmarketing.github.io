Privacy Policy: Carbon Marketing Audit Desktop

Carbon Marketing Audit Desktop is a private tool used only by its owner, Alex Watkinson.

With the owner's permission, it accesses Google Ads account data, Google Docs and Google Drive files in the owner's own Google account. It uses this data only to produce Google Ads audit reports.

Data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google account. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
